Case Studies

E-commerce | Food & Spices

Pak Spices (Peshawar)

Challenge: Business had zero incoming orders and minimal digital presence. Despite quality products, they lacked a customer acquisition strategy and couldn't compete in the online marketplace.

Solution: Built end-to-end Meta Ads campaign from scratch with precision audience targeting, multiple creative variations, and optimized checkout experience. Daily monitoring and bid adjustments ensured maximum efficiency.

Results (Week 1)
Orders acquired: 135 orders
Cost per order: 20.42 PKR (highly efficient)
ROI: Improved to 3:1 (from 1.2:1)
Conversion rate: Scaled 2.5% → 5%
Total ad spend: 2,760 PKR
Travel & Tourism | Activity Booking

Desert Safari Dubai

Challenge: Booking inquiries were minimal despite premium service offering. High-intent customers existed but weren't reaching them through paid advertising. Needed to increase visibility in competitive UAE market.

Solution: Restructured Meta Ads strategy with refined audience targeting (UAE travelers, adventure seekers), compelling video creatives showcasing experience, retargeting campaigns, and optimized landing pages for instant booking/inquiry conversion.

Results (Week 1)
Inquiries/messages: 130 qualified leads
CPM (Cost per 1000 impressions): 0.83 AED (exceptionally efficient)
Total ad spend: 107.98 AED
Lead quality: High-intent, ready to book
Outcome: Consistent booking pipeline established
Local Services | Electronics & Repair

Muqads Electric Store (Lahore)

Challenge: Service repair business had zero online visibility. Despite skilled technicians and competitive pricing, couldn't attract customers through digital channels. Relied solely on word-of-mouth referrals.

Solution: Created targeted Meta campaign focusing on local Lahore audience with service-focused messaging, before/after device repair showcases, and direct messaging optimization for instant service bookings and inquiries.

Results (Week 1)
Service inquiries/bookings: 150 bookings
CPM: 19.18 PKR (cost-effective for local services)
Total ad spend: 2,877 PKR
Lead quality: High-intent, local, ready to book
Outcome: Consistent service flow established; repeat customer rate improving
